Scheduling Algorithms – Descriptions
============================================

1) FCFS (First Come First Serve)
Processes are executed in the order of their arrival time
It is simple and fair with respect to arrival order
Disadvantage: a long process that arrives early can delay all later processes (convoy effect)

2) SJF Preemptive / SRTF (Shortest Remaining Time First)
At each time unit, the ready process with the shortest remaining burst time is selected
It often produces the lowest average waiting time among common algorithms
Disadvantage: needs knowledge (or estimates) of burst times; long jobs may starve

3) Priority Scheduling (Non-Preemptive)
Among ready processes, the highest-priority process is chosen and runs until it completes
In this project, a smaller priority number means higher priority
Disadvantage: low-priority processes may wait a long time (starvation)

4) Round Robin (quantum = 2)
Each process receives a fixed time slice of 2 units. If it does not finish, it goes to the end of the ready queue
It is fair and suitable for interactive systems
Disadvantage: average waiting time is often higher because of frequent context switches
